# 03 - Design System

## 🎨 System Warna & Palette

- **Primary Canvas**: `bg-[#0f172a]` (Slate 900)
- **Card Background**: `bg-[#1e293b]/60` (Slate 800 dengan opasitas 60%)
- **Border**: `border-slate-700/50`
- **Text Primary**: `text-slate-100`
- **Text Secondary**: `text-slate-400`
- **Success / Profit**: `emerald-500` (`#10b981`)
- **Danger / Expense**: `rose-500` (`#f43f5e`)
- **Warning / Hold**: `amber-500` (`#f59e0b`)
- **Accent Brand**: `cyan-500` (`#0ea5e9`)

## 📐 Radius & Spacing
- Container Corner Radius: Maksimal `rounded-2xl` (16px).
- Button Spacing: Padding horizontal 2x dari padding vertikal.
- Spacing Antar Komponen: `gap-3` atau `gap-4`.
