# 10 - Database Schema

## 🗄️ Struktur Database Firebase Firestore

### Koleksi:
1. `accounts`: Rekening bank, e-wallet, cash, & investasi.
2. `transactions`: Catatan transaksi pengeluaran, pemasukan, & transfer.
3. `investments`: Portofolio aset (Saham, Kripto, Emas).
4. `loans`: Catatan piutang (lent) & hutang (borrowed).
5. `savings`: Target tabungan & progres alokasi modal.
6. `attendance`: Log presensi kerja & jam lembur.
